Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how LocalServiceBox ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Bike taxi booking details
  • Order details for local products such as water cans and chicken

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To provide bike taxi booking services
  • To process local product orders and deliveries
  • To communicate service-related updates
  • To improve app functionality and user experience

Phone Number Usage:
We collect your phone number only to enable delivery partners and service providers (such as bike taxi drivers or product delivery personnel) to contact you regarding your order or service. This includes delivery coordination, location clarification, and order updates. This is required for the core functionality of the app.

3. Payment Information

Payments in LocalServiceBox are completed using direct UPI ID transfer. We do not collect, store, or process any bank account details, debit/credit card information, or UPI credentials.

All payments are made directly by users to service providers using their own UPI apps.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your phone number may be shared with delivery partners or service providers only for order fulfillment and service communication purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using third-party services such as Firebase (Google). We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. User Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. You may contact us using the information below.

7. Children’s Privacy

LocalServiceBox is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
